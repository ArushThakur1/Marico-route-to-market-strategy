# Marico Route-to-Market Strategy

**Sales & distribution strategy for Marico: tiered channel architecture, workload-based salesforce sizing, a distributor CRM pipeline and a circular-logistics pilot, built for the omnichannel era.**

---

## 📌 Overview

Marico already reaches 5.8M+ retail outlets through 7,500+ stockists and penetrates 60,000+ villages. The strategic question is not scale but structure: how to reconcile three transitions at once: **direct coverage** (Project SETU, 1M to 1.5M direct-reach stores by FY27), **omnichannel demand**, and **data-driven field operations**.

This project is a strategic diagnostic across four modules, prepared for a live industry audience.

## 🧩 Modules

### 1. Channel Architecture
A tiered distribution intensity by brand, not one blanket rule:
- **Intensive:** Parachute, Saffola (near-ubiquitous kirana availability)
- **Selective:** Set Wet, Livon, Beardo (curated modern-trade and quick-commerce listings)
- **Exclusive (early stage):** Plix and new D2C lines (owned D2C and premium e-commerce first)

**Channel conflict resolution** (Thomas-Kilmann modes):
- General trade vs quick-commerce price friction: *Collaborating* through channel-specific SKUs and pack sizes
- Distributor disintermediation under Project SETU: *Compromising + Accommodating* by giving distributors first right to become logistics partners

### 2. Salesforce Sizing and Quotas (Workload Method)
Modelled on a sample 40,000-outlet cluster:

| Outlet class | Outlets | Visit frequency | Calls per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 6,000 | Weekly (52) | 312,000 |
| B | 22,000 | Fortnightly (26) | 572,000 |
| C | 12,000 | Monthly (12) | 144,000 |
| **Total** | 40,000 | | **1,028,000** |

1,028,000 calls ÷ 2,240 calls per rep per year ≈ **459 reps**.

**Balanced quota mix:** Volume/Revenue 40% · Distribution/Coverage 20% · Activity Metrics 15% · Product Mix 15% · Collections/DSO 10%

### 3. CRM and POS Technology
A seven-stage distributor pipeline:

`Onboard → Credit & Territory → Primary Order → Secondary Sales → Retail Execution → Replenish/Renew → Review/At-Risk`

- **Lead scoring:** prioritises Project SETU rollout outlets by proximity, category-switchability and payment-risk signals
- **Real-time POS loop:** field DMS data syncs nightly; dashboards trigger stock-out alerts and draft replenishment orders
- **Early-warning dashboard:** automatically flags SKU-level price variance between general trade and quick-commerce

### 4. Circular Logistics
Closing the loop through the network Marico already has:
1. **Collect:** distributors and retailers act as informal collection points for empty rigid-plastic packs
2. **Incentivise:** trade-in credit per kg returned, part-funded by existing EPR obligations
3. **Reprocess:** material routed through recycler partners toward recycled (r-PCR) packaging content
4. **Report:** kilograms collected tracked against EPR targets in the sustainability report

Aligned to **UN SDG 12** (Responsible Consumption & Production), **SDG 13** (Climate Action) and **SDG 17** (Partnerships, via the India Plastics Pact model).
